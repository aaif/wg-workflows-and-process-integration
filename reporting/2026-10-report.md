# [Report] Workflows & Process Integration – October 2026
**Working Group:** WG Workflows & Process Integration

**Reporting period:** October 2026

**Chair(s):** Yaron Schneider (Diagrid) & Adam Seligman (Workato)

## Summary

Critical Use Cases got its vote. The use case inventory and classification schema were approved at the 29 Oct plenary, so all three workstreams now have a voted artifact. We also decided to publish our first deliverable as an official white paper and want to bring it to the TC for approval before year-end. Reference Architectures opened a draft of its first multi-agent architecture, and our first set of workflow terms merged into the cross-WG taxonomy on 5 Oct.

## Progress Against Objectives

- **Reference Architectures** (leads: Mario, Zayne)
  - Two syncs, on 6 Oct (6 attendees) and 20 Oct (4)
  - **Single-agent architecture:** PR #53 tightens the wording after Wesley's TC feedback (issue #45), mainly to make clear that "single agent" means one agent asks for approval, not one agent in the whole workflow. The changes are small, so no re-vote. It was ready on 9 Oct along with the "business task" terminology PR (#49), and both merged on 5 Nov
  - **Bounded autonomous remediation:** the minimum-evidence PR (#43) that wasn't ready in September went through three rounds of review with Filip and has converged. Filip signed off on 3 Nov, and it's waiting on a last look from Mario
  - **Primitives:** Praneeth opened PR #54 with 12 primitives (P01 to P12) and a decision record. Primitives make up patterns and patterns make up architectures, so we reference primitives from the pattern documents and leave the architecture files alone. The older topologies PR (#29) is now a draft kept for reference
  - **First multi-agent architecture:** many use cases in the inventory run agents in parallel and then merge the results, so that's what we picked next. In Mario's draft, "Parallel Research and Merge" (PR #56), agents research separate sources without talking to each other, the results are merged into one report that shows what's missing, and a person approves it. It adds two patterns (parallel fan-out and join, branch isolation) and takes about 30 minutes to review. We'd like to hear whether it's useful and readable
  - **After that:** an architecture where agents exchange data. The candidates so far are a producer and reviewer working in a loop, bounded remediation running in parallel across many repositories, and a router that sends each request to the right expert agent. We're describing concepts first and leaving protocols and frameworks open

- **Critical Use Cases** (leads: Kevin, Jodee)
  - Syncs on 20 and 27 Oct
  - **Voted:** the use case inventory (88 use cases across 15 industry categories) and the classification schema passed with a majority at the 29 Oct plenary, after a two-week review window. Only member companies voted, and Adam recorded the voters' names for audit. Both documents describe workflow shapes seen in production deployments and don't recommend anything
  - **Still to land:** the "pattern" to "shape" rename agreed in September didn't make it in before the vote and is now issue #58. Two small PRs are recommended for merge: #47 (fan-out vs. pipeline as an open question) and #48 (broken schema links)
  - Kevin and Wesley are aligned on Wesley's schema feedback (issue #44). The definition of "trigger" goes to Taxonomy
  - A proposed new use case (PR #51) raised a question we haven't answered yet: what makes a use case interesting enough to add? A first-time contributor also proposed a "Boundary" dimension for the schema (issue #60)

- **Taxonomy** (lead: Sumanth, cross-WG liaison: Gala)
  - **Merged upstream:** our initial terms PR merged into the cross-WG taxonomy on 5 Oct. It defines Discovery and Handoff, and adds Activity, Agentic workflow, Agentic lifecycle, Determinism and Orchestration
  - Terms that weren't accepted upstream stay in the WG repo. Sumanth is finishing control flow, trigger and durability there, will then start on a second set, and is planning a GitHub Pages site to display the terms
  - "Business task" still isn't defined anywhere. The plan is to add it to our own terms and link to it from the architectures. "Acceptance record" (from PR #43) may follow
  - The taxonomy stays in GitHub for now. Adam asked how to get it used more widely, including by AI models, and Yaron is looking into how to promote it

## Blockers and Risks

- **Sources in the use case inventory will go stale.** Every entry depends on a public link, and both the links and the pages behind them change over time.
  - *Mitigation:* the 15 Oct plenary agreed to version the artifacts and automate link checks (issue #59, with a first PR, #63, opened on 17 Nov). Yaron is raising versioning of deliverables at the chairs' meeting so working groups handle it the same way.
- **Overlap with other working groups.** Carried over from September. This month it wasn't obvious where a community question about workflow work belonged, and Adam pointed it to Accuracy & Reliability rather than Observability. Model selection is another group's topic, so Use Cases left it out of the schema on purpose.
  - *Mitigation:* cross-WG topics are going through the weekly chairs' meeting. This month that was AI-generated PRs and versioning.
