## Why it is required

- **Pattern that needs it:** which pattern, and which of its invariants
  depends on this primitive.
- **What breaks without it:** the concrete failure in a workflow that has no
  named place for this.
- **Why existing primitives don't cover it:** which ones were checked, and why
  they don't fit.

## Consequences

What changes in the primitive set, the patterns, and any architectures that
reference it. Include renumbering or link updates if any.

## Alternatives considered

of a pattern instead of a primitive is usually one of them.
