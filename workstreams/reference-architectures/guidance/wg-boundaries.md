# Boundaries with other working groups

Per the [WG charter](../../../charter/charter.md) and its out-of-scope list, security and privacy threat analysis, tracing/telemetry/metrics, governance and regulatory frameworks, agent identity and delegation, and reasoning-quality evaluation belong to other AAIF working groups. This RA set defines the interfaces their work plugs into; it does not perform their analysis. This document is the single canonical statement of those interfaces: both [`single-agent.md`](../architectures/single-agent.md) and [`multi-agent.md`](../architectures/multi-agent.md) had their own, near-duplicate "Boundaries with other working groups" section; this file merges them so each working group's interface is stated once, covering both the single-`AgentStep` surface and the multi-agent surface (handoffs, topologies, global invariants).

- **Security & Privacy WG**: *Emits:* tool effect classifications (`read_only` / `reversible_write` / `irreversible`, [boundary contract element 4](../contracts/agent-step-boundary.md)) and identity/delegation scope (element 7) as the surface their threat modelling operates on; and, once a workflow spans more than one `AgentStep`, the [handoff contract](../contracts/handoff.md)'s authority-transfer rule (element 3: no widening without a deterministic gate) as an additional interface. *Consumes:* nothing normative here: their attack-surface analysis of MCP tool-trust boundaries, prompt-injection-class risks, and adversarial agent behaviour across a handoff boundary (see [OWASP Agentic Applications Top 10](https://owasp.org/www-project-agentic-skills-top-10/)) is their output, not an input this RA specifies.
- **Observability & Traceability WG**: *Emits:* the attachment points where traces and metrics instrument (`AgentStep` entry/exit and each `ToolCall`) and, in a multi-agent workflow, the correlation identifiers required by the handoff contract's audit-continuity element, so their tracing can correlate against workflow structure across handoff boundaries as well as within a single step. *Consumes:* nothing structural. [`P15 AuditRecord`](../../taxonomy/primitives/p15-audit-record.md) (step outcomes, decision points, tool invocations, budget consumption, human interventions, and, across a multi-agent workflow, the full execution graph including every handoff) is explicitly distinct from, and does not substitute for, their traces and metrics.
- **Governance, Risk & Regulatory Alignment WG**: *Emits:* the [`P14 OutcomeContract`](../../taxonomy/primitives/p14-outcome-contract.md) termination classification set and the policy-gate/`HumanCheckpoint` decision points as the control points their regulatory mappings attach to: this includes both the single-step confidence-based `Decision` and, at multi-agent scale, the [irreversibility gate (global invariant 7)](global-invariants.md) that places a `HumanCheckpoint` on irreversible or low-confidence outcomes. *Consumes:* which classes of task or tool require a mandatory `HumanCheckpoint` or deterministic gate under a given regulatory regime: this RA leaves that threshold undefined and treats it as an external input, referencing the [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) as the relevant external resource.
- **Accuracy & Reliability WG**: *Emits:* the output contract's confidence/uncertainty signal ([element 3](../contracts/agent-step-boundary.md)) as the artifact their reasoning-quality evaluation operates on, and as the interface that feeds the confidence-based `Decision`/`HumanCheckpoint` routing decision in both single- and multi-agent workflows. *Consumes:* reliability and accuracy thresholds that determine where that routing sends output: to `HumanCheckpoint` or to auto-complete. This RA does not define how confidence is computed or how reasoning quality is evaluated; that is this WG's domain.
- **Identity & Trust WG**: *Emits:* the requirement that every `AgentStep` declare identity and delegated authority ([element 7](../contracts/agent-step-boundary.md)), and, once a workflow spans more than one `AgentStep`, the handoff contract's authority-transfer element as the mechanism this RA assumes exists underneath every `P16 Handoff`. *Consumes:* the actual identity, authentication, and delegation mechanism: this RA specifies that the attribute MUST exist and that authority MUST NOT widen across a handoff without a deterministic gate, not how identity is established or delegation is authenticated.
